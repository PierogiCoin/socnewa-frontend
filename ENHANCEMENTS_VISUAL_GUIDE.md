## 7. 🎨 Color Coding

### Hashtag Competition
- 🔴 **Red** = High Competition (Popular, hard to rank)
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Medium Competition (Balanced)
- 🟢 **Green** = Low Competition (Easy to rank, niche)

### Status Indicators
- 🟢 **Green** = Success, Good, Recommended
- 🟡 **Yellow** = Warning, Moderate
- 🔴 **Red** = Error, Attention needed
- 🔵 **Blue** = Info, Links, Clickable
- 🟣 **Purple** = Primary actions, Enhancements

## 8. ⚡ Interaction Flow

### Complete User Journey
```
1. User writes content
   ↓
2. Clicks "🚀 Enhancements"
   ↓
3. Modal/Panel opens
   ↓
4. Tabs: [🏷️ Hashtags] selected by default
   ↓
5. Clicks "Generate Hashtags"
   ↓
6. AI analyzes (2-3 seconds)
   ↓
7. Results appear:
   - Total reach: 750K
   - Recommended hashtags
   - Strategy breakdown
   ↓
8. User clicks "Add All" or individual hashtags
   ↓
9. Hashtags added to content automatically
   ↓
10. User switches to [�� Templates] tab
    ↓
11. Browses templates, selects "Tips"
    ↓
12. Fills in variables
    ↓
13. Clicks "Apply Template"
    ↓
14. Content is filled/replaced
    ↓
15. User switches to [👁️ Preview] tab
    ↓
16. Sees pixel-perfect preview of LinkedIn post
    ↓
17. Switches platform → Preview updates
    ↓
18. User switches to [⏰ Best Time] tab
    ↓
19. Clicks "Get Best Time"
    ↓
20. Sees: "Tuesday 9:00 AM EST (+45% reach)"
    ↓
21. User closes panel
    ↓
22. Content is ready with hashtags, optimized, previewed
    ↓
23. User clicks main "Generate" or "Publish" button
    ↓
24. Success! 🎉
```
